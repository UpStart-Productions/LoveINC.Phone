J.O.B.S. Program

Are you ready to jump over barriers and land your dream job? The Love INC J.O.B.S. Program is here to empower you with the tools and knowledge you need to succeed in today's competitive job market. Over the course of four enriching evenings, you'll experience 8 hours of targeted coursework to identify your skills, improve your job search, and practice interviewing. You will be offered the opportunity to work at your own computer station to search jobs that match your identified skills and create or upgrade your resume so you will be more marketable in our area's job market.

Why Join the J.O.B.S. Program?

  • Expert-Led Training: Learn from seasoned professionals who know what it takes to succeed.
  • Professional Coaching & Mentoring: Benefit from personalized guidance to refine your skills and strategies.
  • Certificate of Completion: Enhance your resume with a certificate that showcases your commitment and capabilities.
  • Small Class Sizes: Enjoy a focused learning environment that ensures personalized attention and support.

Workshops Include:

  • Transferrable Skills

  • Job Search
