Connection Center

The Connection Center works closely with 23 churches to provide essential services, known as GAP Ministries, that meet needs otherwise unmet in the community. These services include clothing, non-food pantry items, bikes, linens, showers, firewood, and kitchen items and many more. Our regular volunteers are the heart of the Connection Center, listening to people's stories, providing practical assistance, offering financial guidance, and praying with clients, resulting in life-changing moments of peace and support.
