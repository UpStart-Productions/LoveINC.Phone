Get Assistance

The Connection Center partners with 23 churches to meet needs through GAP Ministries—clothing, pantry items, bikes, linens, showers, firewood, kitchen items, and more. Volunteers listen, offer practical help, and pray with clients.

What to expect

  • We'll review your request within 1–2 business days.
  • We'll reach out by phone or email to learn more about your situation.
  • Together we'll connect you with the right support.

Your information is confidential and used only to help you.
